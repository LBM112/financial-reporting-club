About the Club

Reports on the Complex Transactions in Corporate History

What we are

The Financial Reporting Club is an independently run student club that looks in depth at financial transactions, deals and trends, and at how they are priced and reported.

What we write about

We simplify complex financial transactions and make them readable for a wider audience, while judging how viable and reasonable each transaction was.

The nature of the club

We are an open club and anybody is welcome. If you are a reader, you are a member, and everyone is encouraged to get involved. We do not give investment advice, and our articles should not be used to make financial decisions.
